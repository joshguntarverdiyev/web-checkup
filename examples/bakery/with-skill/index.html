Sourdough out of the oven every morning, round the corner.

Crumb & Co. is a small bakery in Lisbon. We bake slow-fermented loaves, a few pastries and pour good coffee. Come by in the morning, or reserve your weekend bread so it's waiting for you.

Bread, pastries & coffee

Every loaf is naturally leavened and fermented for around 36 hours. We use flour from Portuguese mills where we can, plus water, salt and nothing else.
