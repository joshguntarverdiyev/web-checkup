# Privacy Policy

**Web Checkup, Claude plugin directory edition** · Last updated: 7 November 2026

Web Checkup is an open-source Agent Skill: written instructions and reference files for an AI assistant. This edition contains no scripts or other code, and it is not a hosted service.

## What we collect

Nothing. Web Checkup has no servers, accounts, analytics or telemetry. The author receives no data about you, your projects or your use of the skill.

## What your assistant may do while following the skill

The skill tells your AI assistant how to build and review websites. The assistant does this with its own tools and within the permissions you give it:

- **Read your project files** when you ask for a review or audit, for example to look for exposed secrets. The skill tells the assistant never to print full secret values.
- **Request pages from a website you name**, for example to check security headers, `robots.txt` or link-preview tags. These are ordinary requests, the same as visiting the site.
- **Open a website you name in a browser tool** to take screenshots, when such a tool is available.
- **Send a URL you name to Google PageSpeed Insights** for performance checks. The skill tells the assistant to say so when it does.

Google's privacy policy applies to requests sent to PageSpeed Insights. Your AI assistant processes your conversation under its provider's terms; Web Checkup adds nothing to that.

## Retention

Web Checkup stores nothing. Screenshots your assistant takes stay on your computer or in your conversation, depending on the tool.

## Children

Web Checkup is a developer tool and is not intended for children.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this repository with the date above. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/joshguntarverdiyev/web-checkup/issues.
